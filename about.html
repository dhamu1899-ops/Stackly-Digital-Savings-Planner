Our Story

Born from a simple belief.

Stackly began in 2022 when our founders realized that most savings tools felt clinical and uninspiring. Saving money is an emotional journey — one that deserves tools as thoughtful and intentional as the goal itself.

We set out to create a savings experience that combines rigorous financial intelligence with editorial-quality design. Every chart, every notification, every pixel is crafted to make you feel in control of your money.

Today, Stackly helps over 50,000 people build wealth with clarity and confidence, one goal at a time.
